i · Logo

A clock that gives the hour back.

The mark is a clock face with a sun setting on water: the hour of the day you get to keep. It is always drawn, never typed, and it always comes from the master SVG.

  1. i
    The dial. A ring, stroke 3 on a 64 grid, radius 22. Ivory on dark, ink on paper.
  2. ii
    The hands. Minute hand straight up, hour hand at about two o'clock, stroke 2.8 with round caps, pinned by a small hub.
  3. iii
    The sun. The only filled colour, always gold #E3A33B, cut off at the horizon.
  4. iv
    The horizon and water. One long line and two shorter ripples, stroke 2.6.
  5. v
    The wordmark. "spare hour" in lowercase Fraunces Regular, optical size 72, tracked −0.4px. The byline is Inter Semibold caps, tracked 2.6px, in gold.

Do

  • Keep clear space of at least the sun's width on every side.
  • Keep the mark 24px or larger on screen. Under that, use the icon tile.
  • Use ivory strokes on navy or night, and ink strokes on ivory or paper.
  • Write the name as "Spare Hour" in sentences. The lowercase form belongs to the wordmark only.

Don't

  • Recolour the sun, or fill the dial.
  • Put the mark on the creator gradient or on a busy photo.
  • Set the wordmark in any face other than Fraunces, or in caps.
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add a drop shadow to the mark. The one soft shadow on the hero dial is a site detail, not part of the logo.

iii · Type

An editorial serif, a working sans.

Fraunces carries the voice: headlines, numerals, prices and italic asides. Inter does the work: body, buttons and labels. Both are free on Google Fonts.

Display · Fraunces

Aa Hh

Variable, weights 300 to 700 with italics. Headlines sit light, between 320 and 400. Turn optical sizing on so large sizes get the fine-cut forms.

Fallback: Iowan Old Style, Georgia, serif.

Text · Inter

Aa Hh

v · Motion

The hour rewinds once.

The mark has one animation, which plays a single time on page load and never loops. Anyone who has reduced motion turned on sees the mark at rest.

  1. iThe minute hand rewinds a full turn and the hour hand steps back one hour.
  2. iiMeanwhile the sun dips below the horizon and the water dims.
  3. iiiThe sun rises again and the hour hand returns. 3.6 seconds in all, after a 0.4 second pause.

vi · Voice

A neighbour who builds things.

Spare Hour talks like Adrian across a counter: first person, short sentences, plain words. The reader is a busy owner or creator, so every line should save them time.

Short and plain

Short sentences. Name the tool the reader already uses, not the technology behind it.

YesDrafts your everyday replies. Hands you the rest.
NoAn LLM-powered omnichannel response pipeline.

Hours, not features

Lead with the time given back and the job that disappears.

YesA month of posts in twenty minutes.
NoAdvanced AI content generation capabilities.

The owner stays in charge

Say plainly that nothing goes out without approval and the price is agreed first.

YesNothing goes out until you approve it.
NoFully autonomous, set and forget.

The tailoring words, used lightly

Measure, cut, fit, made to measure. Use them in headlines and the process steps, at most one per paragraph.

YesMeasured first. Cut to fit.
NoOur bespoke, hand-stitched, artisanal AI.

Local and personal

Trumbull is named. Adrian signs his own work. Say "I", not "we" or "our team".

YesI build it with you, show you how it runs, and it stays yours.
NoOur team of experts delivers solutions.

No hype

No exclamation marks, "revolutionary" or "game-changing", and no invented numbers. Prices are stated, not hinted at.

YesPlain pricing. Quoted up front.
NoUnlock 10x growth today!
